Legal, privacy and cookies

This launch template is intentionally limited to the behaviour currently implemented by Wandex. It needs operator contact details and legal review before a commercial launch.

Privacy

Wandex stores theme, language, favourites, recent searches and trip plans in the visitor’s browser. The application sends city, coordinate and query information to its listed data providers when a visitor requests data. Wandex does not currently run a configured analytics or advertising script.

Cookies and consent

The current site does not intentionally set non-essential advertising or analytics cookies. Before adding AdSense, analytics or affiliate tracking, implement a consent mechanism appropriate for the visitor’s jurisdiction and load those scripts only after the required consent.

Terms and contact

Travel conditions are informational and source-labelled; they are not safety, medical, legal, visa or booking advice. Operator name, postal address and contact email have not been supplied and must be added before commercial launch.
