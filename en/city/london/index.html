Seasonal planning

Spring

A useful time for parks and long walks, with a light waterproof layer helping keep plans flexible.

Summer

Daylight is long and popular areas can be busy. Reserve key activities in advance and use current conditions to pace outdoor time.

Autumn and winter

Indoor museums and neighbourhood routes work well alongside shorter outdoor blocks. Check rain and wind before crossing the city.
